# SuperQode Mobile Companion: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the SuperQode mobile companion app ("the app"), published by Superagentic AI. Publish this text at https://superqode.dev/privacy.

## The short version

The app does not collect any data. We, Superagentic AI, receive nothing from it: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reports sent to us.

## How the app works

The app is a remote control for SuperQode running on your own macOS computer. It talks only to that computer, over your own Tailscale private network. SuperQode does not run a server or relay between your mobile device and your computer.

- **Your prompts, files and results** travel only between your mobile device and your computer, over an encrypted connection.
- **Your projects, provider keys and job history** stay on your computer.
- **Cloud models.** If you set up a cloud model or agent provider in SuperQode on your computer, it sends prompts and project context to that provider under your own account with them. Their privacy terms apply to that data. Choose a Local only connection when you want a local model to do the work.
- **Tailscale** provides the private network and may relay encrypted traffic. Your use of Tailscale is covered by Tailscale's own privacy policy.

## What is stored on your device

- A device authorization for your paired computer, kept in the system Keychain on this device only. It is not synced and expires after 30 days.
- Unsent drafts (text, file edits and images you attach), kept in protected storage and excluded from backups. You can delete them in Settings.
- Your appearance choice, kept in app preferences.

None of this is sent to us.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code shown by SuperQode on your desktop, and only after you tap Scan. Images reach the app only when you pick them in the system photo picker, and are sent only to your paired computer.

## Your control

You can revoke this device from SuperQode on your computer at any time, disconnect it from the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
