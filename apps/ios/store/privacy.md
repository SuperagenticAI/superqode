# SuperQode for iPhone and iPad: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the SuperQode companion app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), published by Superagentic AI. Publish this text at https://superqode.dev/privacy.

## The short version

The app does not collect any data. We, Superagentic AI, receive nothing from it: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reports sent to us.

## How the app works

The app is a remote control for SuperQode running on your own Mac. It talks only to that Mac, over your own Tailscale private network. SuperQode does not run a server or relay between your phone and your Mac.

- **Your prompts, files and results** travel only between your iPhone or iPad and your Mac, over an encrypted connection.
- **Your projects, provider keys and job history** stay on your Mac.
- **Cloud models.** If you set up a cloud model or agent provider in SuperQode on your Mac, your Mac sends prompts and project context to that provider under your own account with them. Their privacy terms apply to that data. Choose a Local only connection when you want a local model to do the work.
- **Tailscale** provides the private network and may relay encrypted traffic. Your use of Tailscale is covered by Tailscale's own privacy policy.

## What is stored on your device

- A device authorization for your Mac, kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only. It is not synced and expires after 30 days.
- Unsent drafts (text, file edits and images you attach), kept in protected storage and excluded from backups. You can delete them in Settings.
- Your appearance choice, kept in app preferences.

None of this is sent to us.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code your Mac shows, and only after you tap Scan. Images reach the app only when you pick them in the system photo picker, and are sent only to your own Mac.

## Your control

You can revoke this device from SuperQode on your Mac at any time, disconnect it from the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
